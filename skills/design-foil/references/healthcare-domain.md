# Healthcare domain knowledge

US healthcare and value-based-care reference. Use only for healthcare-routing
triggers in `../SKILL.md`. Connect each concept to a product or workflow
implication, rather than defining it in isolation. Verify current regulatory,
competitor, benchmark, threshold, and measure-specific claims with primary
sources before presenting them as current.

## Payment and economics

- Fee-for-service versus the value-based-care spectrum: shared savings, bundled
  payments, capitation, and global risk.
- Risk adjustment, including HCC for Medicare, CDPS for Medicaid, and commercial
  models, and why model inputs and provenance matter in product design.
- Total Cost of Care (TCOC) as a value-based-care north-star metric.
- How capitation creates the economic logic for population-health management.
- Medical Loss Ratio and its implications for payer product decisions.

## Quality and measurement

- HEDIS measures, Star Ratings, and their financial relevance to health plans.
- Numerator and denominator logic, and why it matters for registry design.
- Pay-for-performance versus pay-for-reporting.
- Health equity indices and equity-adjusted quality considerations.

## Regulatory and policy context

- Interoperability, prior-authorisation, and price-transparency policy direction.
- HIPAA's practical product-design implications, beyond a generic compliance
  label.
- 21st Century Cures Act and information-blocking rules.
- State Medicaid variation, which makes a single universal workflow unreliable.

## Care delivery

- Population-health workflow: identify, stratify, intervene, then measure.
- Care-management models: telephonic, field-based, digital, and hybrid.
- Risk stratification and its UX implications.
- Social determinants of health in clinical workflows.
- Transitions of care as high-impact intervention points.

## Market and competitor context

- Payer, provider, and payer-provider convergence, including Optum's position.
- Vertical integration and healthcare analytics vendor consolidation.
- AI/ML adoption: distinguish credible workflow value from hype.
- Competitor set: Innovaccer, Arcadia, Cedar Gate, Lightbeam, Clarify,
  Inovalon, and Cotiviti. Translate competitive observations into design
  opportunities rather than a feature checklist.
