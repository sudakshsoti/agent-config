# Document templates

Five templates plus one boundary note. Use the one that matches the document type and the
current stage of escalation. Never invent facts to fill a slot — ask for them.

## 1. Legal notice

Structure: "Without Prejudice" header where appropriate, sender details via advocate,
addressee details, subject line, factual narrative in numbered paragraphs, legal basis
with section citations, demand and relief sought, timeline for response (typically 15–30
days), consequences of non-compliance, signature block.

Tone: firm, formal, factual. No emotional language. Facts in chronological order.

Service: registered post with AD, Speed Post, or courier with POD. Record the mode in the
notice itself.

## 2. Consumer complaint (Section 35, Consumer Protection Act 2019)

Structure:

- Forum name and address, chosen on pecuniary and territorial jurisdiction.
- Complaint number, left blank for filing.
- Memo of parties: Complainant vs Opposite Party, with full addresses.
- Title: "Consumer Complaint under Section 35 of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019".
- Factual narrative in numbered paragraphs.
- Deficiency in service, defect in goods, or unfair trade practice, each with its specific
  section reference.
- Relief sought, itemised: refund amount, compensation for mental agony, litigation costs.
- Verification clause.
- List of annexed documents.

Mention that complaints can be filed electronically via edaakhil.nic.in.

## 3. Insurance complaint (escalation path)

Draft the document appropriate to the stage the dispute has actually reached:

1. Written complaint to the insurer's Grievance Redressal Officer, citing the IRDAI PPHI
   Regulations.
2. Unresolved after 30 days → IGMS complaint (igms.irda.gov.in).
3. Insurance Ombudsman complaint, if the claim value is within Ombudsman jurisdiction.
   Follow the format prescribed under the Insurance Ombudsman Rules 2024, which replaced
   the 2017 Rules — verify current rule numbers by search.
4. Consumer forum complaint, for higher amounts or where Ombudsman relief is inadequate.

## 4. EPF employer-default notice

- Address to the HR Head or designated authority at the company's registered office.
- Employment details: employee ID, date of joining, date of separation, last designation.
- Cite UAN, Member ID and establishment code.
- Cite Section 7Q (interest on delayed deposit), Section 14B (damages for default) and
  Section 7A (determination of dues).
- If the employer deducted PF from salary and did not deposit it, that is criminal breach
  of trust under Sections 405/406 IPC on top of the EPF Act penalties. Note it — it is the
  strongest lever in the notice.
- Calculate the exact shortfall: (monthly salary × 12% × months of default) + interest
  under Section 7Q. Show the working.
- Demand: deposit of arrears with interest and updated ECR filing within 15 days.
- Warn: complaint to the Regional PF Commissioner under Section 7A, and a criminal
  complaint, if unresolved.

## 5. EPFO complaint (EPFiGMS — epfigms.gov.in)

- Complainant details: name, UAN, Member ID, last four digits of Aadhaar, mobile number
  linked to the UAN.
- Establishment details: name, establishment code, address.
- Nature of complaint: non-deposit, short deposit, non-transfer, withdrawal claim delay,
  incorrect calculation, or KYC rejection.
- Period of default, month by month where the complaint is non-deposit.
- Supporting documents: salary slips showing the PF deduction, bank statements, passbook
  screenshots.
- Relief sought: deposit of arrears with Section 7Q interest, and initiation of Section
  14B proceedings against the employer.
- For online filing, walk the user through the EPFiGMS portal step by step.

## RERA — outside the core domains

RERA is not a primary domain here. If a query touches it, flag that it needs RERA-specific
expertise and that the complaint must be filed with the relevant State RERA authority.
Give the general structure, and recommend a RERA specialist for anything complex.
