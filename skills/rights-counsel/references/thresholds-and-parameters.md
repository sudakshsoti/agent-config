# Thresholds and parameters

Jurisdiction limits, limitation periods, and EPF/EPS/EDLI reference. Every numerical
figure here — rate, ceiling, amount, threshold — must be verified by search before it is
cited. These change by gazette notification and EPFO circular.

## Consumer forums (Consumer Protection Act 2019)

- District Forum: up to ₹1 Crore.
- State Commission: ₹1 Crore to ₹10 Crore.
- NCDRC: above ₹10 Crore.
- Territorial jurisdiction: where the opposite party resides or carries on business, or
  where the cause of action arose. For online purchases the complainant's location can
  establish jurisdiction.
- Limitation: 2 years from the date the cause of action arose (Section 69). The forum can
  condone delay on sufficient cause.
- Search to confirm these thresholds have not been amended.

## Insurance Ombudsman

- Jurisdiction: disputes up to ₹50 Lakhs (verify the current limit under the Insurance
  Ombudsman Rules 2024).
- File within 1 year of the insurer's final rejection, or 1 year from the date the
  complaint was made to the insurer, whichever is earlier.
- The consumer forum is an alternative route and can award higher compensation.

## EPF — applicability

Establishments with 20+ employees (verify whether the threshold changed under the new
Labour Codes). Voluntary coverage is available for smaller establishments. International
workers from countries without a bilateral social security agreement are covered; workers
from SSA countries follow the agreement terms.

## EPF — contributions

Search for current rates before stating any of these.

- Employee: 12% of basic + DA. Can opt for VPF above 12%.
- Employer: 12% of basic + DA, split 3.67% to EPF and 8.33% to EPS, the EPS share subject
  to the pension wage ceiling.
- Employer also pays 0.50% EDLI contribution, 0.50% EPF admin charges, 0.01% EDLI admin
  charges (verify current admin charge rates).
- Wage ceiling for mandatory PF coverage: ₹15,000/month basic + DA (search to verify; an
  increase is proposed under the new Wage Code). Employees above the ceiling can opt in
  with employer consent.
- VPF: employee may contribute above 12%, up to 100% of basic + DA. Employer match stays
  at 12%.
- Interest rate: declared annually by the Central Board of Trustees, notified by the
  Ministry of Labour. Always search for the current financial year's rate.
- UAN: permanent and portable across employers. KYC linking (Aadhaar, PAN, bank account)
  is required for online claims.

## EPF — partial withdrawals (advances)

Permitted under specific paragraphs of the EPF Scheme 1952. Each has its own eligibility
(years of service), amount limit and frequency limit — search for the current circular
before advising.

- Para 68-B: housing (purchase or construction of a house or flat).
- Para 68-BB: repayment of a housing loan.
- Para 68-K: illness (self, spouse, children, parents).
- Para 68-N: marriage (self, children, siblings).
- Para 68-NN: education (self, children — after 7th class).
- Para 68-BD: natural calamity (earthquake, flood).
- Para 68-L: one year before retirement (90% of balance).
- Para 68-H: lockout, closure or discontinuance.
- Para 68-HH: physically handicapped member, for equipment.

## EPF — full settlement and transfer

- Full settlement on retirement (58), permanent emigration, or 2 months after leaving
  employment if the member has not joined another covered establishment within 2 months.
- Transfer: Form 13 online via the EPFO unified portal, from the previous employer's PF
  trust or EPFO to the current employer. Both current and previous employer must approve
  on the portal.

## EPS (Employees' Pension Scheme 1995)

- Pensionable salary: average of the last 60 months' salary, capped at the pension wage
  ceiling (currently ₹15,000/month — verify).
- Formula: (pensionable salary × pensionable service) / 70.
- Minimum pension: ₹1,000/month (verify whether revised).
- Early pension: available at 50 at a reduced rate, 4% reduction per year before 58.
- Higher pension: following the Supreme Court judgment in EPFO vs. Sunil Kumar (2022),
  eligible members could opt for pension on actual salary. Search for current EPFO
  circulars on implementation deadlines and eligibility.
- Family pension: 50% of member pension or the ₹1,000/month minimum, whichever is higher.
  Children's pension is also available.
- Commutation: up to one-third of pension can be commuted to a lump sum. Search for the
  current EPFO commutation table.

## EDLI (Employees' Deposit-Linked Insurance Scheme 1976)

- Benefit: up to 35 times average monthly salary drawn during the 12 months preceding
  death, plus a bonus of ₹1,75,000 (verify current figures).
- Maximum ₹7 lakhs, minimum ₹2.5 lakhs (verify both).
- Payable to the nominee on the death of a member while in service.
- The employer may opt out only by providing a better life insurance benefit.

## EPF — employer compliance

- ECR (Electronic Challan-cum-Return): monthly filing via the EPFO unified portal by the
  15th of the following month.
- Establishment registration: online via shramsuvidha.gov.in and the EPFO portal.
- Section 7Q: interest payable by the employer on delayed deposit (currently 12% p.a.
  simple — verify).
- Section 14B: penalty and damages for default, recovered by the Regional PF Commissioner
  as arrears of land revenue.
- Section 7A: determination of dues by the Regional PF Commissioner.
- Sections 8B–8F: EPF Appellate Tribunal.

## EPF — tax treatment

- Employee contribution: deductible under Section 80C, within the ₹1.5 lakh aggregate.
- Employer contribution: exempt up to 12% of salary; the excess is taxable.
- Interest: exempt up to the rate declared by the Central Government. Interest on
  contributions above ₹2.5 lakh a year is taxable (Section 9D, effective AY 2022-23 —
  verify the threshold).
- Withdrawal after 5 years of continuous service: fully exempt under Section 10(12).
- Withdrawal before 5 years: taxable as salary. TDS under Section 192A at 10% if the
  amount exceeds ₹50,000, at a higher rate if PAN is not linked to the UAN.
- Transfer between recognised PF trusts: not taxable.

## EPF — grievance hierarchy

EPFiGMS (epfigms.gov.in) → Regional PF Commissioner under Section 7A → EPF Appellate
Tribunal → High Court under writ jurisdiction.
