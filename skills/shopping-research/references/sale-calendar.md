# Sale calendar and timing

Indian retail timing. Derive the current season from today's date — never hardcode a year.

## Major sales

| Event | Window | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Flipkart Big Billion Days | Sep–Oct, around Navratri/Dussehra | Everything; deepest electronics discounts of the year |
| Amazon Great Indian Festival | Sep–Oct, runs alongside BBD | Everything; matches Flipkart closely |
| Diwali sales | Oct–Nov | Appliances, gold, home; brand-store offers as well as marketplaces |
| Republic Day | Jan | Electronics, appliances, fashion |
| Independence Day / Freedom Sale | Aug | Electronics, mid-tier discounting |
| End of Season Sale (EOSS) | Jan–Feb and Jun–Jul | Fashion and footwear, in-store and online |

## Monthly and recurring

- Bank card days — SBI, HDFC, ICICI and Axis run rotating instant-discount days on Amazon
  and Flipkart, typically 10% capped at ₹1,500–3,000. Check the current running offer;
  they change every quarter.
- No-cost EMI is near-universal in festive windows on anything above ₹10,000. It is
  usually the discount repackaged — compare the cash price before taking it.
- Exchange bonuses on phones and appliances are stacked on top of the sale price and are
  often the largest single lever.
- Platform flash sales run all year and rarely beat a festive price.

## Category timing

- **Electronics and appliances** — bottom out during Sep–Oct festive sales. New phone
  launches drop the previous generation sharply about two weeks after launch.
- **Air conditioners and coolers** — cheapest Nov–Jan, most expensive Apr–Jun. Buying in
  the heat costs the most.
- **Fashion and footwear** — EOSS in Jan–Feb and Jun–Jul. Massimo Dutti and similar
  mid-luxury are worth buying on sale only.
- **Fragrance** — no real Indian sale cycle. Sephora India and Nykaa Luxe run occasional
  15–20% events; Dubai duty-free beats both year round for niche.
- **Home and furniture** — festive and Republic Day; also the tail of EOSS.

## The rule

Two to three weeks out from a major sale, recommend waiting and estimate the saving. Just
after one, warn that prices are elevated and will not move for weeks. If nothing is
imminent, say so and recommend buying now.
