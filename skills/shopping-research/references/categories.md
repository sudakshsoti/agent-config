# Category factors and after-sales

## What matters per category

| Category | Decide on |
|---|---|
| Electronics and tech | Real-world performance over spec sheets, software update record, service density, how the Indian model differs from the international one |
| Home and kitchen | Durability, space needed, power draw, voltage compatibility, spares availability |
| Fashion and apparel | Indian versus international sizing, fabric quality, return policy, seller authenticity |
| Beauty and personal care | Skin type, ingredients, shelf life, counterfeit risk |
| Fragrance | Format and concentration, climate suitability, batch age, decant before a full bottle |
| Fitness and sports | Build quality, floor space, noise, whether it survives an Indian summer |
| Baby and kids | BIS/ISI certification, age suitability, toxicity standards |
| Automotive | Compatibility with the specific Indian model, ease of fitting, warranty effect |

## Service network

For anything above ₹10,000, after-sales is a named factor in the recommendation, not a
footnote.

- Compare service centre density in metros against tier-2 and tier-3 cities.
- Samsung, LG, Whirlpool, Bajaj and Voltas have wide networks. Newer entrants such as
  Dreame and Roborock often rely on courier-based service — a two-week turnaround.
- State whether service is on-site or carry-in.
- Flag brands with a poor Indian after-sales record even when the product is good.
- Mention extended warranty options (Amazon Protect, brand plans) and whether they pay
  for themselves at that price.

## Budget bands

Ask for a budget rather than assuming one. Where the user has given bands before, respect
them and do not nudge upward by default. Menswear bands are in
[`channels.md`](channels.md). Where budget is open, offer tiers and let the user pick.

## Warranty and import

An imported unit usually has no Indian warranty. Say so before comparing prices. For a
formal import, note the customs duty (typically 18–44% depending on the category) and
whether the brand honours a global warranty in India — most do not.
