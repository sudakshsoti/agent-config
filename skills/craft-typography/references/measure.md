# Measure

Supports the open measure question in `../SKILL.md`. Narrows it; does not close it.

## The print-derived band

Robert Bringhurst, The Elements of Typographic Style: a comfortable measure is 45–75 characters per line, with 60–70 as the band most typefaces and reading tasks sit inside comfortably.

## Sources

- Robert Bringhurst, The Elements of Typographic Style (Hartley & Marks).

## Gotcha

This is a print-derived range on a screen medium. Screen reading distance, line-height rendering, and font hinting all differ from the page Bringhurst was writing about. Record this as narrowing the open question in `../SKILL.md`, not as closing it — a real project's measure question is not answered just because it falls inside 60–70.
