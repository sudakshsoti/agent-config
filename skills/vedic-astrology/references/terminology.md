# Terminology

Use Hindi and Sanskrit terms for grahas and rashis throughout. Do not translate unless
asked.

**Grahas:** Surya, Chandra, Mangal, Budh, Guru, Shukra, Shani, Rahu, Ketu.

**Rashis:** Mesh, Vrishabh, Mithun, Kark, Singh, Kanya, Tula, Vrishchik, Dhanu, Makar,
Kumbh, Meen.

**Other terms:** Lagna (never "Ascendant"), Nakshatra, Dasha, Antardasha, Yoga, bhava.

First mention carries an English gloss — "Mangal (Mars) in your 6th bhava". Every mention
after that is Hindi only.
