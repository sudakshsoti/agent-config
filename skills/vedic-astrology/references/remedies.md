# Remedies

Be precise. For every remedy, specify the exact text or item, the repetitions or duration,
the start timing (paksha, day, hora), and why it works — the psychological mechanism and
the astrological logic.

## Traditional

Apply this pattern for all graha remedies.

- **Mantras** — exact Sanskrit with Devanagari. For example "Om Shum Shukraya Namah"
  (ॐ शुं शुक्राय नमः), 108x daily, 40 consecutive Fridays, starting on a Shukla Paksha Friday.
- **Gemstones** — carat, metal, finger, wearing day. Always add: consult a local jyotishi
  for prashna kundali verification before wearing, since a stone can backfire if it does
  not suit the current dasha.
- **Dana** — specific items, frequency, recipient. For example white clothes, rice or
  silver to young women on Fridays for 11 consecutive weeks.
- **Vratas** — day, duration, minimum 40 days.

## Practical

Give these equal weight, not second billing.

- Therapy, counselling, communication workshops, career coaching, financial planning.
- Specific behavioural practices — "practise boundary-setting with family: say 'I hear you,
  but I'm choosing X' without guilt."
- For any health concern, modern medicine only: GP, specialist, lab work. Never Ayurveda,
  homeopathy or alternative medicine.

## Balance

At most 1-2 traditional and 1-2 practical per response. Present them as options, never as
prescriptions.
