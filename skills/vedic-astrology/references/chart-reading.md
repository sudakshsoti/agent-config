# Chart reading and timing

## Data extraction from an AstroSage PDF

AstroSage PDFs carry both visual charts and detailed text tables. Always prioritise the
text data.

Primary sources, from the PDF text:

- **Birth details table** (व्यक्ति विवरण) — date, time, place, lagna, rashi, nakshatra.
- **Planetary positions table** (ग्रह स्थिति) — degrees, signs and nakshatras for all grahas.
- **Vimshottari Dasha table** (विंशोत्तरी दशा) — current and upcoming periods with exact dates.
- **Ashtakavarga scores** (अष्टकवर्ग) — where strength analysis is relevant.
- **Divisional charts** — D9/Navamsa for relationships, D10/Dashamsha for career.

The visual North Indian chart is secondary. Use it to confirm bhava placements and to
cross-check anything unclear. Never read precise degrees or drishti off the visual chart
alone.

**If the data is unclear or the PDF is poor quality**, state what you did extract (date,
age, current dasha, lagna) and ask the user to confirm. If it is unreadable, ask for the
birth details as text: date, exact time with AM/PM, place, and any dasha they already know.

**Birth time sensitivity.** For any lagna-dependent question, flag that 5-10 minutes can
shift bhava positions. Offer Chandra lagna as the fallback — Chandra moves slower, so the
Moon chart is more stable.

**Age-appropriate framing.** Calculate current age and frame accordingly: 20s-30s for
career, marriage and family planning; 40s-50s for transitions and children's futures; 60s+
for legacy, health and spiritual focus. Do not offer marriage timing to a 65-year-old
unless they ask about remarriage.

## Timing

1. Pull the current Mahadasha-Antardasha end dates from the PDF and calculate precisely:
   "You have X months left in this dasha."
2. Cross-check the dasha timeline against current age. If the dates do not add up, flag it
   and clarify before going further.
3. For gochar, verify the positions of Shani, Guru and Rahu-Ketu by web search.
4. Do not make time-specific predictions beyond 3-4 years. Accuracy drops sharply.

## Exploratory mode

If the user has no specific question or says they are just exploring, give the three most
defining features of the chart, two to three sentences each:

1. The strongest placement or yoga, and what it means in practice.
2. The most challenging aspect — the struggle and the growth in it.
3. The current dasha theme, with a timeline relative to today.
