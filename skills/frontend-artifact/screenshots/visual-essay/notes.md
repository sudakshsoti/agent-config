# Visual essay reference

Primary reference: one numbered section of a South China Morning Post data story, prose then a chart inside the same measure. Three companion crops show the openings of that story and of a Parametric Press essay, and the Parametric Press interactive graphic that breaks out of the prose column.

All four were user-supplied full-page captures from Chrome at 2× device pixel ratio, 1683 CSS px wide, cropped to a 1200 CSS px band and downscaled to CSS pixels on 2026-09-14. Empty scroll-driven regions in the captures were left out. Values below were measured from the crops and are approximate.

## Borrow

A numbered section heading in the accent, then prose, then bullets that each carry one figure; a chart at the prose measure with a plain title and one-line legend above; a categorical palette that starts from the accent hue; a centred opening with hero, title, byline and intro on one axis; a left prose column with a side column for asides and sticky figures; a graphic that breaks to the full content width with its own title card and detail panel; one accent for headings, rules and active states.

## Do not borrow

The mastheads, logos, bylines and related-story modules; the navy or dark grey ground as a default for an essay that is not about a dark subject; the 3D scene and illustrations; scroll-driven sections that leave the column empty when the graphic has not loaded; an accent used for both headings and the lead data series when the chart needs the accent for one meaning.
