## Borrow

Entries with a consistent metadata order; full descriptive titles rather than truncated ones; images at their own proportions; a status or availability line per object; topic filters that read as text.

## Do not borrow

The site's dark overlay, modal and cookie treatment, oversized institutional navigation, or image cards without real object metadata and a browse or search purpose.
