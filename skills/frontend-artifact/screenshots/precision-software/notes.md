## Borrow

A compact two-line row with identifier, title and secondary line; time and status right-aligned in a fixed column; the panel heading kept at row-title size; state shown by opacity and a dot, never by a badge.

## Do not borrow

The dark treatment as a default, a marketing demo as a functional app, or Linear's brand. Keep the working surface compact and purposeful.
