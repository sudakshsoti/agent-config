## Borrow

A module as a labelled function with title, description and diagram inside one outline; diagrams as black linework with labelled leader lines; the same outline weight and radius on every module; one small navigational link per row.

## Do not borrow

The brand's exact illustrations, product marks, the cookie overlay, or an oversized title when the artifact needs dense reading. Keep the clarity and the physical-instrument character.
