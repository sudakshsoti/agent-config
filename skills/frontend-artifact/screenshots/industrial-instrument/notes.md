## Borrow

A mid grey or near-black ground with text one or two steps lighter and rules one step lighter than the ground; a line drawing of the object in the text colour, centred and wider than the prose; a numbered index with the number in its own narrow column and no rules; outlined cards with no fill for parts and accessories; full-bleed table rows with a fixed pitch and one baseline per row; a column pitch that repeats across tabs, table columns and cards; one colour per row meaning one product; a panel dashboard with a bordered panel one step above the page, a bold title with an icon, a grey subtitle and stacked bars at the panel's inner width; two-column definition lists for facts.

## Do not borrow

The teenage engineering masthead, wordmark, Japanese blurb and icon set; the product photographs; the store's centred heading as a page opener; the guides table's colours as decoration when rows have no product identity; Radar's promotional banner, blog cards and the sidebar; the panel grid when there are fewer than three panels' worth of readings; a light grey weight at 30px on a mid grey ground when the artifact's body text must be read at length.
