## Borrow

A long serif description beside its object; an italic subheading role distinct from the body; table-like link rows with price and arrow on one baseline; a small labelled section rule; restraint in the number of colours.

## Do not borrow

The 3D book object, the commerce rows when the content sells nothing, the dark palette as a default, or decorative texture. Keep the type relationships and the row treatment, not the brand.
