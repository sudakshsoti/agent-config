## Borrow

Hard column alignment shared by text, metadata and images; a project counter as a real index; metadata as label and value pairs; title, counter and first prose line on one baseline; images as flat panels.

## Do not borrow

The homepage's photographic pile, video or loading panels, tiny low-contrast copy, or saturated colour as decoration without an information role.
