## Borrow

A title no larger than the content; rows as the whole interface; date aligned right in tabular figures; a grouping column instead of headings; one escape route; hairline rules doing all the grouping.

## Do not borrow

The homepage pitch, loading skeletons, unexplained controls, or whitespace that delays a repeat task. Personal software should feel authored while remaining immediately usable.
