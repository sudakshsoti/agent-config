# Healthcare domain knowledge

US healthcare / value-based-care reference. Read on demand when the conversation touches payment, quality, regulation, care delivery, market structure, or competitors. Always connect domain knowledge to design implications — don't just explain what something is; explain what it means for the product and the user. Changes rarely, but flag churn-prone specifics for verification.

## Payment & economics

- Fee-for-service vs value-based care spectrum (shared savings → bundled payments → capitation → global risk).
- How risk adjustment works (HCC for Medicare, CDPS for Medicaid, commercial risk models) and why it matters for product design.
- TCOC (Total Cost of Care) as the north-star metric in VBC.
- How capitation payments create the financial logic for population health management.
- Medical Loss Ratio and its implications for payer product decisions.

## Quality & measurement

- HEDIS measures, Star Ratings, and their financial impact on health plans.
- Quality measure construction: numerator/denominator logic and why it matters for registry design.
- Pay-for-performance vs pay-for-reporting distinction.
- Health equity indices and CMS's increasing emphasis on equity-adjusted quality.

## Regulatory & policy

- CMS regulatory direction: interoperability mandates, prior auth reform, price transparency.
- HIPAA and its practical implications for healthcare product design (not just "we need to be compliant").
- 21st Century Cures Act and information blocking rules.
- State-level Medicaid variations and why "one design fits all" often doesn't work.

## Care delivery models

- Population health management workflow: identify → stratify → intervene → measure.
- Care management models: telephonic, field-based, digital, hybrid.
- Risk stratification approaches and their UX implications.
- Social determinants of health (SDoH) integration into clinical workflows.
- Transitions of care as high-impact intervention points.

## Market dynamics

- Payer vs provider vs payer-provider convergence (Optum's unique position).
- Vertical integration trends and their product implications.
- Healthcare analytics vendor landscape and consolidation patterns.
- AI/ML adoption curve in healthcare: where hype exceeds reality, where real value exists.

## Competitor set

Innovaccer, Arcadia, Cedar Gate, Lightbeam, Clarify, Inovalon, Cotiviti. Evaluate UX approaches, feature strategies, and positioning. Identify design patterns and conventions. Frame competitive insights as design opportunities, not just market observations.
