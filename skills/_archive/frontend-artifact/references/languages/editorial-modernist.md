# Editorial modernist

## Fit and job

Use for arguments, case studies, strategy and research narratives where sustained reading carries the work. The page should feel typeset: hierarchy, text rhythm, captions and purposeful image placement organise the material. Compared with quiet catalogue, the primary sequence follows an argument rather than a collection of entries.

These defaults authorise visual choices within the requested content and behaviour; they do not authorise new features or changes to the artifact's job.

## Defaults

- Composition: establish one clear reading sequence with a controlled prose measure. Let supporting imagery, evidence and captions interrupt or sit beside that sequence where the content calls for them. Use asymmetry only when the reading order remains clear.
- Density: give sustained prose enough space for comfortable reading while keeping captions and supporting material visibly attached. Vary spacing at actual changes of topic or pace, not between every paragraph.
- Typography roles: create a deliberate contrast between headline, body and supporting roles through cut, scale or proportion. Pair families only when their roles need different drawings; a well-chosen family can carry the page. Treat captions, notes and quotations as real reading roles.
- Palette roles: begin with a readable text and background relationship and a restrained accent for navigation or editorial emphasis. Let meaningful imagery contribute colour without forcing every section into the same decorative palette.
- Grouping and surfaces: use alignment, paragraph rhythm, whitespace and rules. Let sections read as a document rather than a sequence of rounded panels.
- Interaction: keep links, references and requested reading controls clear and restrained. Avoid entrance animations or scroll effects that obstruct the reading sequence.

## Three distinguishing features

1. The prose establishes the page's main reading order.
2. Headline, body and caption roles have visible contrast without every role changing all its properties at once.
3. Imagery and supporting evidence punctuate the reading sequence rather than occupying a uniform card grid.

## Permitted variation

Display type and asymmetric composition are available when they serve editorial reading; neither is compulsory. The type system may be serif-led, sans-led or use complementary families, subject to inspected font files and licences. A sparse essay and a dense research narrative can share this language while using different spacing and supporting structures.

## Avoid

- A serif headline on cream as the entire editorial decision.
- Pull quotes, invented statistics or oversized headings added to fill space.
- Magazine-like columns that produce an unclear reading order on a phone.
- Applying a long-form opening to a utility or dashboard merely because this language was selected.

## Visible review criteria

- Reading order is clear at every target width, including when side material moves into the main flow.
- Body text has a comfortable measure, and captions remain recognisably attached to their images or evidence.
- Type contrast expresses content roles rather than competing for attention across the whole page.
- Every large gap, image placement and display treatment has a visible pacing or hierarchy purpose.
