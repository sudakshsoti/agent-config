## Borrow

A greeting or title barely larger than the content; equal columns that each hold one kind of thing; entries as a bold name over a plain description with one blank line between; dates inline or right-aligned, never as their own heading; one accent mark that is not text; link cards sized to their content; an escape route at the foot of each list; rows as the whole interface with hairline rules doing the grouping; a year column instead of headings; a second face used once.

## Do not borrow

The dark panel-on-ground framing as a default (either ground alone is enough); the 40ch columns for prose longer than a summary; the orange dot as a logo; the italic serif for anything but the escape route; the shop and typeface cards as page furniture; loading skeletons, unexplained controls, or whitespace that delays a repeat task. Personal software should feel authored while remaining immediately usable.
