## Borrow

A byline and date in a small monospace or sans above a serif title; one full-width rule separating title from standfirst; a standfirst at about 1.6× body; a wide measure only with tight leading; a hairline that marks the prose's first baseline; figures at the full prose measure with a small monospace caption and a right-aligned credit; one section heading size barely larger than body; sidenotes aligned to the line they annotate in a smaller sans, with one colour reserved for reference marks.

## Do not borrow

The publications' illustrations, logos and issue framing; the 80–85ch measure when the body leading is 1.5 or more; Works in Progress's monospace as a brand voice rather than a meta role; Asterisk's oversized blue headline stack when the artifact is not a magazine feature; the figure's photographs; the "Similar pieces" and contact footer as page furniture. Keep the type roles, the rule and column relationships, and the caption and sidenote treatments.
